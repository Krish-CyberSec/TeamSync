# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

TeamSync welcomes respectful participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels. We are committed to making project participation professional, constructive, and free from harassment.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It is guidance for community behavior, not a guarantee of any particular moderation capacity or response time.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate of other people and their experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback professionally.
- Focus discussion on the work, evidence, and project goals.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and correct their impact where possible.
- Respect privacy and do not disclose another person's private information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or targeted abuse.
- Sexualized language, unwanted sexual attention, or explicit material in project spaces.
- Insults, personal attacks, trolling, or repeated disruptive conduct.
- Publishing private or identifying information without permission.
- Other conduct that a reasonable person would consider harmful or unprofessional in a project community.

## Scope

This code applies in TeamSync repository spaces, including issues, pull requests, code review, and project discussions if enabled. It also applies when someone is representing the project in a public setting.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to **K.singh.codebase@gmail.com**. Do not report conduct incidents in public issues if that would disclose personal or sensitive information. For security vulnerabilities, use the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not this contact placeholder.

Reports should include a description of the behavior, where and when it occurred, and any relevant context. Share only information needed to review the concern. The maintainer will review reports as practicable and respect reporter privacy to the extent possible, subject to safety and applicable law.

## Enforcement

The repository owner or designated maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code. No independent moderation team or formal response SLA is currently established.

Depending on severity and context, enforcement may include:

1. A private correction or warning.
2. Removal or editing of content, or rejection of a contribution.
3. Temporary restrictions on participation.
4. Permanent exclusion from project spaces and future participation.

The maintainer may take proportionate action immediately for serious conduct. Enforcement decisions should be fair, consistent, and communicated when appropriate. A person reported for misconduct should not be the sole reviewer of the report.
